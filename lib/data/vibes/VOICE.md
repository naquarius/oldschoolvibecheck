# Vibes voice guide

How to write the vibes-mode reading for each hexagram (`en.ts`, `zh.ts`). Classic mode carries the source text; vibes mode is the fast, plain answer for someone with a real question and a short attention span.

## Shape

Each hexagram has three fields:

| Field | Shown as | Length | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| `vibe` | the big line | 2–5 words (中文: 4–9 characters) | the feeling or situation in one breath |
| `realTalk` | one line right under the vibe, above the hexagram | 1–2 short sentences, ≤ 25 words (中文: ≤ 40 characters) | what this means for you right now |
| `theMove` | "the move", after the hexagram | 1–2 short sentences, ≤ 25 words (中文: ≤ 40 characters) | one concrete thing to do |

## Rules

1. **Stay true to the judgment.** Every reading starts from what the hexagram's judgment actually says (`gua-data.ts`). If the classic says "follow, don't lead", the vibe can't say "take charge".
2. **Don't quote the source.** No "the text says", no "the old text", no retelling the classical image (the fox, the mare). Use the meaning, not the picture. Classic mode is where the source lives.
3. **Talk to the person, about now.** Second person, present tense, plain words. Readable in one glance.
4. **One slang touch at most.** A single "rn", "lowkey", "fr" or "full send" is fine. No stacked memes.
5. **Nothing that dates or borrows.** No celebrities, brands, games, astrology (mercury retrograde), or other divination traditions.
6. **Lowercase.** Same as the rest of the app.
7. **The move is doable today.** Something specific the person can actually do, not "stay positive" or "trust the process".
8. **No fake certainty.** Readings guide; they don't promise outcomes ("you will get the job").
9. **中文 is written, not translated.** Write from the Chinese judgment in natural modern Mandarin with a light touch of 网络用语; it doesn't have to match the English word for word.

## Example

#64 Before Completion · judgment: success, but a little fox that nearly finishes crossing gets its tail wet; nothing furthers.

- vibe: `don't slip at the finish`
- realTalk: `you're like 90% there, and that's exactly where people get sloppy and lose it.`
- theMove: `lock in for the last bit. check the thing you think is already done.`

Not: `don't fumble at the finish line` / `you're so close to legendary status… secure the bag properly.` (stacked slang, generic), and not: `the text says a little fox…` (quotes the source).
